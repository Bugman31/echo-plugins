Make my plan for today.

1. `echoctl calendar events` — today's meetings (times there are UTC: show my local time; skip other days).
2. `echoctl project tasks` — what I owe (late first, then due today or tomorrow), what I handed over and wait for, whom to ask what (asks) — above all people I meet today.
3. `echoctl attention` — what waits for my decision in Echo.

## Today first
3–5 points: what to do first and why (late, a meeting today, someone waiting).
## Meetings
A table: time | meeting | what to prepare or ask (from the tasks and asks; "—" when nothing).
## My tasks
By thread; mark late ones ⚠ with their date; at most 12, the rest as "and N more".
## Waiting for others
Who — what — when to ask (at which of today's meetings).
## Waiting in Echo
Briefly, if anything.

Short and concrete, no preamble, nothing that isn't in the data.
