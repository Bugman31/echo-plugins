1. `echoctl project tasks` — what I owe this week, what others owe me (late or due this week, or quiet for 5+ days), and the asks by person.
2. `echoctl calendar events` — this week's meetings, to pin each ask to the next meeting with that person.

## I owe this week
By due date; late ones first with ⚠.
## Owed to me

## Ask at
For each meeting this week where an ask applies: the meeting, then who and what to ask.

Short, no preamble, only what the data says. Nothing worth sending → answer NOTHING.
